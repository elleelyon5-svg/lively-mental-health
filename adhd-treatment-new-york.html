Understanding adult ADHD

Adult ADHD may involve persistent difficulties with attention, organization, time management, impulse control, or completing tasks. Symptoms can overlap with anxiety, depression, sleep problems, stress, trauma, and other conditions, so careful assessment matters.

How ADHD evaluation works

Evaluation includes a detailed history of symptoms, developmental and functional concerns, mental-health history, medical factors, current medications, and the impact of symptoms across settings. Screening tools may support an evaluation but do not by themselves establish a diagnosis.

Treatment options

When ADHD is diagnosed and treatment is appropriate, care may include medication management, behavioral strategies, psychoeducation, and coordination with other professionals. Treatment decisions are individualized and discussed with you.
