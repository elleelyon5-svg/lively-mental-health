What is psychiatric medication management?

Medication management is an ongoing process of evaluating psychiatric symptoms, reviewing current medications, monitoring response and potential adverse effects, and adjusting treatment when clinically appropriate.

A personalized approach

Medication decisions are not one-size-fits-all. Your history, symptoms, previous treatment, other medications, medical conditions, preferences, and treatment goals are considered when developing a plan.

Follow-up visits provide an opportunity to discuss how you are doing, whether treatment is helping, and whether changes or additional support should be considered.

Conditions and concerns

Medication management may be considered as part of care for adults experiencing concerns such as depression, anxiety, and other psychiatric symptoms when evaluation indicates that medication treatment may be appropriate.
