Virtual mental-health care designed around you

Lively Mind and Wellness provides psychiatric services through telehealth for eligible adults located in New York. Virtual appointments can make care more accessible for people balancing work, family responsibilities, travel, or other demands.

Services available through telehealth

Services may include psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and psychotherapy, depending on clinical appropriateness.

What you need

You will need a private setting, a compatible device, a reliable internet connection, and the ability to participate safely in a telehealth visit. Additional requirements may apply based on the service and your clinical needs.

Who can receive care?

Services are intended for eligible adults who are physically located in New York at the time of care and meet clinical and legal requirements. Telehealth is not appropriate for every person or every situation, and an initial assessment helps determine whether the practice is an appropriate fit.

Private-pay practice

Lively Mind and Wellness does not bill insurance directly. Current fees are posted on the practice fees page. A superbill may be available upon request for potential out-of-network reimbursement.
