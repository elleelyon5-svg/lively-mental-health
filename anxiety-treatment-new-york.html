When anxiety starts getting in the way

Anxiety can affect concentration, sleep, relationships, work, physical comfort, and your ability to feel present in everyday life. Symptoms can vary from persistent worry and restlessness to panic, irritability, or difficulty relaxing.

How Lively Mind and Wellness approaches anxiety

Care begins with an individualized assessment. We consider your symptoms, history, current medications, health factors, stressors, and goals before discussing treatment options.

Depending on the assessment, care may include psychotherapy, medication management, behavioral strategies, or referral to another professional or level of care when appropriate.

Telehealth anxiety care in New York

Lively Mind and Wellness provides telehealth services for eligible adults located in New York, subject to clinical and legal requirements. Virtual care can make it easier to attend appointments from a private, convenient setting.

Private-pay care

Lively Mind and Wellness is a private-pay practice. Current fees include a $200 initial psychiatric evaluation, $100 medication-management follow-up, and $175 psychotherapy visit.
