What psychotherapy can offer

Psychotherapy provides a structured space to talk through emotional and behavioral concerns, understand patterns, develop coping strategies, and work toward personally meaningful goals.

Areas of support

Lively Mental Health offers individual psychotherapy for adults experiencing concerns such as anxiety, depression, stress, burnout, life transitions, self-esteem challenges, and women's mental health concerns.

Your treatment goals are discussed during the assessment, and the approach is tailored to your needs and clinical presentation.

Online therapy in New York

Eligible adults located in New York may receive psychotherapy through telehealth when clinically appropriate. Virtual care allows you to participate from a private setting that works for your schedule.

Psychotherapy and medication management

Some people benefit from psychotherapy alone, while others may benefit from a combination of psychotherapy and medication management. When clinically appropriate, these services may be discussed as part of a coordinated treatment plan.

Private-pay fee

The current psychotherapy fee is $175 per visit. Lively Mental Health does not bill insurance directly. A superbill may be available upon request for potential out-of-network reimbursement.
