What is a psychiatric evaluation?

A psychiatric evaluation is a comprehensive assessment of your current concerns, symptoms, health history, medications, psychosocial factors, and treatment goals. At Lively Mind and Wellness, the evaluation is designed to understand the whole person rather than focus on a single symptom.

What to expect

Your initial appointment may include discussion of mood, anxiety, sleep, concentration, stress, relationships, work or school demands, previous treatment, medications, substance use, medical history, and other factors relevant to your care.

When clinically appropriate, the evaluation may lead to a diagnostic impression and a personalized treatment plan. Treatment options can include psychotherapy, medication management, lifestyle and behavioral recommendations, referrals, or a combination of approaches.

Who may benefit?

Adults located in New York who are seeking professional help for concerns such as anxiety, depression, stress, burnout, life transitions, or other mental-health symptoms may request an evaluation. Clinical appropriateness is determined through the intake process.

Private-pay care

The current fee for an initial psychiatric evaluation is $200. Lively Mind and Wellness does not bill insurance directly. A superbill may be available upon request for potential out-of-network reimbursement, which is determined by your insurance plan.

Related services
