FAQ

Before you begin.

Do you accept insurance?

No. Lively Mental Health is a private-pay practice and does not bill insurance directly.

Can I submit a superbill to my insurance?

You may request a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Your insurance plan determines eligibility and reimbursement; reimbursement is not guaranteed.

Is care provided virtually?

Yes. Telehealth services are available for eligible patients located in New York, subject to clinical and legal requirements.

Do I need a referral?

No referral is required by the practice. Clinical appropriateness and intake requirements still apply.

What if I am experiencing an emergency?

Lively Mental Health is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or believe you may harm yourself or someone else, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. You may also call or text 988 in the U.S.
