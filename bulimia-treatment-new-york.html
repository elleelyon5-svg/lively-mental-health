Support for Bulimia Nervosa in New York

Bulimia nervosa is a serious eating disorder that can affect mental and physical health. Psychiatric care may be part of a coordinated treatment plan when outpatient telehealth is clinically appropriate.

Understanding bulimia nervosa

Bulimia nervosa can involve recurrent episodes of binge eating followed by behaviors intended to compensate for eating, such as self-induced vomiting, fasting, or excessive exercise. The condition can have significant medical and psychiatric consequences.

Comprehensive assessment matters

Assessment considers eating patterns, compensatory behaviors, nutritional and medical concerns, mood and anxiety symptoms, medications, substance use, and safety. Coordinated care with a primary-care clinician, eating-disorder specialist, dietitian, or another level of care may be appropriate.

When telehealth may not be appropriate

Telehealth is not appropriate for every person with an eating disorder. Medical instability, significant dehydration or electrolyte concerns, severe nutritional compromise, an acute psychiatric emergency, or another urgent concern may require prompt in-person or emergency evaluation.

Individualized psychiatric care

When outpatient telehealth is clinically appropriate, psychiatric care may address co-occurring anxiety, depression, sleep concerns, medication needs, and other mental-health symptoms as part of a coordinated treatment plan.

Private-pay care

Lively Mind and Wellness is a private-pay practice. The current initial psychiatric evaluation fee is $200. Final treatment recommendations depend on the clinical assessment.

Next step

If you are an adult located in New York and want to discuss whether an evaluation is appropriate, you can schedule a consultation. If you have signs of a medical emergency, seek urgent in-person care rather than waiting for a telehealth appointment.
