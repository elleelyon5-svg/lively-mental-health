Understanding bipolar disorder

Bipolar disorders involve episodes of mood disturbance that may include periods of depression and episodes of mania or hypomania. Symptoms, patterns, and treatment needs vary from person to person.

Comprehensive psychiatric assessment

Evaluation may include mood history, sleep and energy changes, thought and behavior changes, depressive symptoms, substance and medication history, medical factors, family history, and functional impact. The goal is to distinguish bipolar symptoms from other conditions that can produce overlapping experiences.

Medication management and ongoing monitoring

When medication treatment is appropriate, ongoing care focuses on response, side effects, safety, adherence, and changes in mood or functioning. Treatment may also include psychotherapy, lifestyle and sleep support, and coordination with other clinicians.

Telehealth bipolar care in New York

Lively Mind and Wellness provides telehealth services for eligible adults located in New York, subject to clinical and legal requirements. Acute mania, psychosis, severe depression, or other urgent concerns may require a higher level of in-person care.
