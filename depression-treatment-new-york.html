Understanding depression

Depression can affect mood, motivation, sleep, appetite, concentration, relationships, work, and enjoyment of activities. Symptoms can look different from person to person, and a careful assessment helps determine what may be contributing to how you feel.

Individualized assessment and treatment

At Lively Mental Health, care begins with a psychiatric evaluation that considers symptoms, history, medical and medication factors, previous treatment, current stressors, and personal goals.

Depending on your assessment and clinical needs, treatment may include psychotherapy, medication management, behavioral strategies, or referral for additional services when appropriate.

Telehealth in New York

Eligible adults located in New York may receive services through telehealth when clinically and legally appropriate. Virtual appointments offer a convenient way to participate in care without traveling to an office.

Private-pay fees

The current initial psychiatric evaluation fee is $200. Psychotherapy is $175 per visit and medication management is $100 per follow-up. Lively Mental Health does not bill insurance directly.

If you are in crisis

Lively Mental Health is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or believe you may harm yourself or someone else, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. In the U.S., you can also call or text 988.
